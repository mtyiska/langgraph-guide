# Project Atlas

## Overview

Project Atlas is a data platform initiative to centralise internal reporting across all business units.
The platform will serve 200 internal users across finance, operations, and product teams.
The project began Q1 2024 with an approved budget of $320,000 across two phases.

## Goals

The primary goal is to reduce report generation time from 3 days to under 4 hours.
Secondary goals include improving data accuracy to 99.9% and enabling self-serve analytics for non-technical users.
The platform integrates with existing ERP system SAP and CRM system Salesforce.

## Success Criteria

Success is defined as 80% of target users generating reports independently within 60 days of launch.
A secondary metric is reduction in data team support tickets by 40%.
